Fish Audio logos

The Fish Audio logo files in this folder are Fish Audio's official brand assets. They belong to Fish Audio,
come from the logo package linked on https://docs.fish.audio/developer-guide/resources/brand, and are not
part of Lobe Icons (LICENSE-lobe-icons.txt does not cover them).

fishaudio-lockup-white.svg  FishAudio-Horizontal-White.svg, unchanged
fishaudio-lockup-black.svg  FishAudio-Horizontal-Black.svg, unchanged
fishaudio-icon-white.svg    FishAudio-Horizontal-White.svg cropped to the icon
fishaudio-icon-black.svg    FishAudio-Horizontal-Black.svg cropped to the icon

The icon files only set the root <svg> width, height and viewBox to 118 x 44, the icon's own area. The artwork is
not changed; everything outside that area is cut off.

Fish Audio's rules: do not distort, rotate, recolour or add effects to the logo. Use the white file on dark
backgrounds and the black file on light ones; prefer the lockup (icon and wordmark) and use the icon alone only
where space is too tight.
