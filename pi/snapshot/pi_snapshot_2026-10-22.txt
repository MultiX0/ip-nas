Snapshot of my NAS Pi on 2026-10-22, the setup from the video: a fresh Raspberry Pi OS Lite (trixie), CasaOS 0.4.15,
Nur Hub, SearXNG, Nextcloud and Jellyfin from CasaOS, and the screen's Pi agent. Taken over SSH with read-only commands;
each header is the command. IP addresses, Wi-Fi names, user names, serial numbers, UUIDs and passwords are left out.

On this day the drives were still mounted by CasaOS's devmon under /media/devmon and the apps still kept everything on
the SD card. pi/README.md mounts the drives at /DATA/HDD1 and /DATA/HDD2 and puts the app data there.
